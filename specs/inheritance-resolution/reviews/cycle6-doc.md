## Correction 3 -- clarifying paragraph on why #86 can't cover this

**Verification run:**
- Read `src/flextoolsmcp/server/handlers/api.py`:
  - `_direct_ancestors()` (lines 449-457): returns
    `entity.get("interfaces", []) + entity.get("base_classes", [])` --
    confirms the walk is over `interfaces` UNION `base_classes`.
  - `collect_inherited_members()` (lines 474+): docstring confirms it "walk[s]
    `entity_name`'s ancestry" via BFS over `_direct_ancestors`, i.e. strictly
    upward/ancestor-only; `entity_name`'s own members are explicitly excluded
    and merged in separately by callers.
  - This mechanism is structurally incapable of surfacing a member declared
    on a *descendant* (`IMoStemMsa`) when walking from an *ancestor*
    (`IMoMorphSynAnalysis`) -- confirms the technical claim in the requested
    paragraph.

**Edit applied** (inserted into "Why its own issue, not folded into #86",
now lines 79-86, immediately after the existing paragraph):
```
It's also not something #86's mechanism could ever close: `MsFeaturesOA`
is declared on the concrete subtype `IMoStemMsa`, not on any ancestor
interface, while `collect_inherited_members()` (added by #86 in
`13f69f8`) only walks ancestors upward via `interfaces` union
`base_classes`. An upward ancestor walk can never surface a member
declared on a descendant, so #86's merge does not, and structurally
cannot, address this case -- it needs a downcast edge, which is a
different mechanism entirely.
```
Two sentences, as requested; no new section header added (folded into the
existing "Why its own issue, not folded into #86" section).
